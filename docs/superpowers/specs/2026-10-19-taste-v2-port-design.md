# taste-skill v2 → dev-flow: what we take, what we fix, what we decline

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Asked by:** the user ("voglio il lavoro completo e fatto bene con criterio")
**Source studied:** [`Leonxlnx/taste-skill`](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) v2.1.1 (MIT), read in full at
commit `b9ca2fd`: `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md`, its 12 references, `library/`, `scripts/`, `evals/`, CI.

## Why

`design-md-to-app/references/anti-slop-fallbacks.md` was adapted from taste-skill **v1**. v2 is a different product
(a script deals an art direction, a real-browser inspector measures the page, a screenshot critique loop scores it).
Comparing it line by line against our skills found three kinds of thing:

1. **Places where taste is right and we are wrong** — some of them contradictions inside our own files.
2. **Ideas we have no equivalent for**, cheap to adopt and compatible with our golden rules.
3. **Things we decline**, because they break golden rules 2 (i18n) and 3 (the primitive renders the design), or
   because the evidence does not support them.

## Decisions

### Decline (recorded so nobody re-proposes them)

- **Depending on taste-skill, or vendoring it.** It is a second design authority beside DESIGN.md; its page mode
  fails stock shadcn primitives (borders, `Separator`, Card shadow, ~10px radius); its type and colour live in
  parallel CSS variables off the shadcn/Tailwind scale; it has no i18n and its copy lint is English-only; its gate
  says "do not argue with the meter" where shadscan says read the code and never optimise the score. Its own blind
  test (`references/research.md` §11) scored the skill at or below the no-skill baseline in 4 of 5 rounds, judged by
  a model. It stays a suggestion in `external-skills.md`, for one case.
- **The motion floor** (a page FAILs under 8 moving elements). A count is gamed by eight hover transitions and says
  nothing about purpose; `transitions` keeps "every motion names a purpose".
- **The picture floor** (≥ 4 real pictures) and the page-mode layout bans as gates. They are taste's house style.
- **Blanket reduced motion** (`transition-duration: .01ms` on everything). Ours stays "fewer and gentler, not zero".

### Fix (taste is right)

- **F1 · Invented proof.** Rule 3 asks for "organic" statistics (`47.2%`, `3,847 members`) — fake proof that reads as
  real, which is how it ships. Split: **sample data** (rows in a dashboard, demo profiles) stays realistic and is
  logged; **proof** (customer counts, ratings, testimonials, logos, press, business statistics on a public page) is
  **never invented** — the slot ships empty-with-a-TBD or is left out, and the hand-off lists it.
- **F2 · Our own contradictions.** Rule 6 offers a "numbered vertical list" (rule 11 bans 01/02/03) and "horizontal
  scroll on mobile" (a row cut at the screen edge reads as broken). Rule 9 says `tw-animate-css` ships a shimmer
  (`transitions/references/tw-animate-css.md` says it does not); `transitions` lists "skeleton shimmer" while rule 11
  bans decorative gradients — the skeleton is `animate-pulse` (opacity), never a moving gradient sweep. Rule 10's
  stagger starts at `opacity-0` with no reduced-motion path: content stays hidden.
- **F3 · The generator watermark.** design-md-to-app always writes "Generated by design-md-to-app from
  .workflow/DESIGN.md" in `font-mono text-[11px] uppercase` into the client's public footer. It goes; the provenance
  lives in `_design-md-mapping.json` and `/showcase`, which are ours.
- **F4 · Off-scale and untranslated instructions.** The hero (`text-[40px] lg:text-[64px]`), the nav and footer
  (`text-[14px]`, `text-[11px]`) and the mobile checklist prescribe literals our own lint rejects; both shipped
  templates (`site-top-nav`, `wordmark-footer`) hard-code Italian strings. They move to the redefined `--text-*` slots
  and to `useTranslations`.
- **F5 · Placeholder pictures.** Picsum-with-seed is a random photo with extra steps. Order of preference becomes: the
  project's own → a real fragment of the product built from its own primitives (software) → drawn in code from the
  subject → type and colour alone → Picsum-with-seed **only as a logged draft**, listed for replacement before launch.

### Take (no equivalent today)

- **T1 · The page argument, before the public area is drawn** (`wireframe-canvas`). For every screen whose roles
  include `visitor`: an inventory of 8–12 things only this client has; the idea sentence ("On this page, X becomes Y")
  with the **competitor test** (put the nearest competitor in the sentence — if it still works, the idea is the
  category's); the **argument** (one claim per section with its proof; a section with no claim is cut); the one
  live thing (optional); product fragments as real primitives, never grey bars. Written to
  `docs/wireframes/page-argument.md` with fixed English labels (the content is in the user's language), checked by
  `wireframes.py argument`, and required by `wireframes.py approve` when the inventory has a public screen
  (`--no-argument "<reason>"` records an exception). The area review uses a short marketing-look checklist.
- **T2 · Dashboard numbers and charts** (`anti-slop-fallbacks.md`, a new rule): a delta's colour says good/bad, not
  up/down, and a zero or undefined change is neutral ("+4 (new)", never "+100%"); figures right-aligned with
  `tabular-nums`; one figure in one place; at most four chart hues taken from the `--chart-*` tokens, one accent and
  neutrals; no gradient area fill; a direct label beats a legend; an empty section keeps its frame and heading.
- **T3 · Copy shape** (same file): headline shapes that are statements, not slogans; the English copy tells added to
  the banned words — still subordinate to the brand voice.
- **T4 · Two lines per headline, per locale** (`i18n-next-intl.md` and the page argument): Italian runs 15–30% longer
  than English, so a headline is fitted to the **longest** locale at 390 px, and a forced break is a `t.rich` tag,
  never a `<br>` baked into one language.
- **T5 · A render probe** (`app-verification/scripts/render_probe.mjs`): what no tool of ours measures because
  shadscan and `check.py` read source. It opens a URL in headless Chrome at 1440 / 768 / 390 and reports: horizontal
  overflow with the elements causing it; headings over two lines; text that stays invisible with reduced motion on;
  a declared font that never loaded; broken images; a sidebar taking over 30% of a phone. Zero dependencies (Node ≥ 22
  for the built-in WebSocket, an installed Chrome/Chromium/Edge), **no local file server** (taste's serves the whole
  working directory, `.env` included — the probe takes the dev server's URL or a `file://` path), a throwaway profile
  removed on exit, nothing written outside `--out`. A measurement: exit 0 unless `--strict`. Wired into
  design-md-to-app's 375 px verification, transitions' Audit mode and the verify manual's Evidence.
- **T6 · `check.py` follows the rules it grades:** `invented-proof` (copy in code and `messages/*.json`),
  `generator-watermark`, `hidden-without-reduced-motion`, `chart-gradient-fill`; `round-stat`'s note no longer
  recommends organic-looking proof.
- **T7 · `external-skills.md` row** for taste-skill: only for a public marketing site with no DESIGN.md, Figma or
  preset whose owner wants award-level art direction; free (MIT) but ~30–45 min and a few hundred thousand tokens a
  build (its README), Node 22 + Chrome; its rules fight shadcn defaults; run its inspector against a URL.

## Constraints

- Golden rules: code in English; i18n en + it on every frontend; the primitive renders the design, type inside the
  shadcn/Tailwind numeric scale; `@shadcn/lint` stays clean.
- Every rule in `anti-slop-fallbacks.md` still **bows to the source** (Step 4.5c): Figma/DESIGN.md win.
- Credit: ideas adapted from taste-skill (MIT, © Leonxlnx) are credited where they land; no text is copied.
- Existing projects are not migrated: `approve` is the only new requirement and it runs once per project.
